Contact your admin

Ask your library administrator to add you from the admin panel using your official @xu.edu.ph or @my.xu.edu.ph email (student assistants may use their student address). You will receive a temporary password and should change it after first sign-in.

Student assistants can sign in to the staff portal with a dedicated staff account on @my.xu.edu.ph, or keep only a student account and have the administrator grant “reservation desk” access instead.
